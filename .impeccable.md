## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are contractors and permit applicants using the product to get clear, reliable building-code guidance for Tampa projects. They need quick confidence while preparing permit submissions and project decisions.

### Brand Personality
Premium, credible, and efficient. The interface should feel professional and trustworthy while still being fast to scan and use.

### Aesthetic Direction
Data-dense product UI with polished surfaces, precise spacing, and strong information hierarchy. The product supports both light and dark themes with equal visual quality.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize confidence: important system status and next actions must be obvious at a glance.
2. Design for fast scanning: concise copy, strong hierarchy, and compact but readable layouts.
3. Keep interactions predictable: clear controls, explicit feedback, and minimal friction through each workflow.
4. Preserve visual discipline: consistent spacing, typography, and component rhythm across screens.
5. Treat both themes as first-class: no loss of contrast, clarity, or polish in either mode.
